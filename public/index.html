About

A new chapter in Arab media

Rania Barghout is a renowned media personality, television presenter, producer, and executive communication coach. The Next Chapter is her own media company: podcasts, production, media training, and public speaking development under one roof.

Its first release, No2ta A Al Satr, marks her return to the spotlight.

With more than three decades in television and hundreds of interviews with public figures, experts, and leaders, Rania now helps individuals and brands share their stories with clarity, confidence, and impact.
